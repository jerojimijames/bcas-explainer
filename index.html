Thermal imaging camera
DETECTION MODULE — COMPONENT 1

The camera is the system's eyes. It continuously rotates 180°, scanning the airspace using infrared thermal imaging. Because it detects heat rather than visible light, it operates in complete darkness, fog, rain, or any weather condition 24/7 with no human intervention. All frames are fed in real time to the detection software on the control module.

Acoustic module
DETERRENCE MODULE — COMPONENT 2

The acoustic module is mounted on a motorised rotating platform. When the control module identifies and tracks a bird, it commands the platform to physically rotate toward the target and emit a proprietary acoustic signal. This signal triggers an Acoustic Startle Reflex (ASR) — an involuntary neurological response — forcing birds to change direction. Unlike conventional noise cannons or distress calls, birds cannot habituate to the ASR signal over time.

Control module
INDUSTRIAL PC + DETECTION SOFTWARE — COMPONENT 3

The brain of the entire system. An industrial-grade PC runs Volacom's proprietary detection engine, object classification algorithm, and motion tracking software. It processes the thermal camera feed in real time, distinguishes birds from other heat sources, calculates the target's trajectory, and issues timed commands to the acoustic module and rotating platform. All data is stored locally and served over Ethernet to the web dashboard.

Continuous thermal scanning

The thermal camera rotates at a configurable speed (6–12 seconds per 180° sweep) and sends a continuous stream of infrared frames to the detection engine on the industrial PC. The 640×4096 resolution allows simultaneous tracking of multiple birds across the full panoramic field of view. Scanning never stops — even during an active deterrence event.

KEY PARAMETERS
Scan rate: 6–12 seconds per 180° sweep
All-weather: zero visibility, rain, night
Multi-target simultaneous detection
Feeds directly to detection engine
Proprietary detection & classification

Each thermal frame is analysed by Volacom's proprietary detection software. The algorithm identifies heat signatures matching the size, shape, and movement profile of avian species. Non-bird objects (vehicles, aircraft, humans) are classified separately and do not trigger deterrence. The software has been trained and validated across numerous species with 100% deterrence success in field tests.

KEY PARAMETERS
Distinguishes birds from false positives
Validated across multiple species
Detects average bird at 500 m+, large species at 1 km+
Runs on industrial PC in real time
Real-time position tracking

Once a bird is confirmed, the system continuously tracks its position, heading, and speed using frame-by-frame analysis. This tracking data is used to predict the bird's trajectory and calculate the precise angle at which the acoustic module must be pointed for effective deterrence. Multiple birds can be tracked and prioritised simultaneously based on proximity to protected zones.

KEY PARAMETERS
Position, heading, and speed computed
Trajectory prediction for lead-targeting
Multi-bird prioritisation by proximity
Updates continuously per frame
Targeted acoustic deterrence

The rotating platform receives a direction command from the control module and mechanically rotates the acoustic module to face the target. The proprietary signal — an ASR (Acoustic Startle Reflex) waveform — is then emitted in a focused ±15° beam directly toward the bird. The signal triggers an involuntary neurological reflex, causing the bird to immediately change course. Because it bypasses learned behaviour and targets the brainstem reflex arc, the bird cannot become habituated to it.

KEY PARAMETERS
Motor rotates to exact azimuth angle
Emits ASR waveform — involuntary reflex
Effective range up to 500 m (BCAS 10.0)
Zero harm to wildlife — tested by WWF
Event logging & database storage

Every detection event is automatically logged with a video clip, timestamp, GPS-referenced location, species classification, and deterrence outcome. This data is stored in the control module database and immediately accessible through the web dashboard. Operators can filter, search, export, and analyse millions of records for compliance reporting, risk assessment, and operational review.

KEY PARAMETERS
Video record per detection event
